About

I finished my undergrad in Computer Science and Engineering at CUET in August 2025, and a few weeks later I was standing in front of a classroom as a Lecturer at Northern University Bangladesh — which is a faster turnaround than I expected, and I'm still getting used to being called "Sir" by people roughly my own age.

Most of what I care about research-wise sits somewhere between NLP and medicine — right now that means hierarchical disease classification from patient conversations, and separately, trying to understand how bias creeps into language models and what, if anything, we can actually do about it. Neither project is finished. Both are currently sitting in review queues, which is its own kind of patience exercise.

— Aninda
